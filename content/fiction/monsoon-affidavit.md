I, the undersigned rain,
do solemnly affirm:

that I entered the city without a permit,
that I assembled unlawfully on every roof,

that I redacted the mountain from the window
and published the river in the street;

that I held the whole town in custody for an afternoon
and released it, smelling of earth, without a charge;

that the umbrellas filed their objections
and I overruled them, gently, all;

and that whatever I have flooded I have also fed —
signed, in a hundred million small marks,

wherever your paperwork
was left out in the open.
