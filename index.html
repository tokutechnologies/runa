§01

The brief

Who is filing

I am a lawyer first. Human rights are not abstractions to me — they are case files with names: a detainee’s cell, a migrant’s contract, a survivor’s testimony — and my work is to put them on the record.

An Advocate licensed to practise in Nepal, I have since 2019 served as a Human Rights Officer at the National Human Rights Commission of Nepal, working across the Legal Affairs Division: investigating violations in the post-conflict landscape, preparing and presenting case briefs before the Commission’s bench, monitoring prisons, detention centres and elections, and reviewing the laws that are supposed to protect people.

I hold an LL.M. in Human Rights and Gender Justice and a B.A.LL.B specialised in Criminal Law from Kathmandu School of Law. I plead on behalf of the Commission in the courts of Nepal, draft its recommendations to Government, and carry its voice into rooms from Kathmandu to Bangkok.
